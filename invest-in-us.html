Building the Next Contemporary Indian Design House

India possesses extraordinary depth in craft, architecture, materials, making traditions and cultural narratives, yet much of this remains disconnected from contemporary homes and modern lifestyles. NAD exists at this intersection — translating cultural capital into relevant, functional and collectible design.

Our opportunity is to build a recognisable Indian design house with multiple commercial engines spanning products, spaces, projects, experiences and digital commerce.
