Serious, skill-focused learning for dedicated practitioners, designers and craft lovers. Unlike beginner workshops, Masterclasses require serious learning intent and prior experience may be required.

1. Botanical Dyeing — Intensive
Level: Intermediate to Advanced Duration: 2 Days Batch: Max 6
Deep dive into natural dye extraction, mordanting textiles, eco-printing, and permanent pigment binding.
Prerequisites: Basic understanding of natural fibers.
Outcome: Master natural vat dyeing, mordant recipes, and custom fabric palette creation.
Instructor: Master Textile Artisan
2. Linocut & Printmaking — Intensive
Level: All Serious Learners Duration: 2 Days Batch: Max 8
Precision gouging, multi-block registration, custom ink mixing, and hand-press printing on handmade rag paper.
Outcome: Create limited-edition relief prints and master registration tooling.
3. Cyanotype — Intensive
Level: Intermediate Duration: 1 Day Batch: Max 8
Emulsion chemistry mixing, UV exposure tuning, toning variations (tea/coffee/tannin), and printing on wood/fabric.
4. Mirror Mosaic — Intensive
Level: Intermediate Duration: 2 Days Batch: Max 6
Heritage Indian Lippan and Thikri glass craft. Geometric layout, mud-plaster prep, precision glass cutting, and framing.
5. Furniture Restoration & Reimagining (Advanced NAD Program)
Level: Advanced / Designers Duration: 3 Days Batch: Max 4
Structural joinery repair, antique timber preservation, distressing techniques, and transforming vintage items into contemporary luxury pieces.
6. Reclaimed Wood Product Making & Surface Development
Level: Intermediate to Advanced Duration: 3 Days Batch: Max 5
Sourcing reclaimed teak/rosewood, hand-planing, organic oil finishing, and crafting bespoke functional decor objects.
